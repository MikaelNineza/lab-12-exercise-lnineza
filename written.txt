Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
    The bounding boxes get bigger because the they cover the same grouping
    of boxes that keep going further apart from each other.
Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
    What happens is that the bounding boxes no longer keep growing
    because we remake the grouping of each bounding box so it always
    has circles that are close to each other.
Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
    When drawing a circle, we don't take into account the radius,
    so that means that it's possible for a circle center to be on
    one of the sides of the screen and therefore have a part of the circle
    past the screen.
